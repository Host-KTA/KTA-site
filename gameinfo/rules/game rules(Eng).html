game rules

Basic Rules


Deck Construction

A deck is built with a total of 30 cards, consisting of Follower Cards and Spell Cards, centered around 1 Character Card.

(As of 2026, the current meta heavily relies on Spell Cards, with Follower Cards being very limited in number and Spell Cards making up more than 90% of the deck.)



Card Types

Character Card


A card that serves as the center of battle. Although it does not directly participate in combat, its effects have a significant impact on the overall game.

The character's affiliation is displayed in 🟦 at the top left.

The character's HP is displayed in 🟨 at the bottom right.

When a character's HP reaches 0 or below, the player loses the game.

Depending on the Character Card's rank, the character has between 30 and 36 HP.


Follower Card


A card that attacks enemy Followers or directly attacks the opponent's Character during battle.

The Follower's affiliation is displayed in 🟦 at the top left.

🟨 at the bottom displays the Follower's Attack / Defense / HP in order.

The Follower's Size is displayed in 🟥 at the top right.

When an opponent's Follower is destroyed, the opponent's Character loses HP equal to the Size of the destroyed Follower.

When directly attacking the opponent's Character, the opponent loses HP equal to the Size of the attacking Follower.


Spell Card


The Spell's affiliation is displayed in 🟦 at the top left.

The Spell's Size is displayed in 🟥 at the top right.

On the Field, Spell Cards always are activated before Follower Cards.



Game Progression

First Step. Initial


Initiative is determined by a coin toss on first turn. After the first turn, Initiative can be checked by the Character's dialogue.

In initial turn, "Initial" effect of cards will activate.


Second step. Standby

on standby Phase, players can place any cards from your Hands onto the Field.

However, "the total combined Size of cards placed on the Field" cannot exceed 10.


Third step. Battle

Battle priority is determined by a coin toss

Red (1st) / Blue(2nd)


During battles, each card on the Field takes action once.

If both Follower Cards and Spell Cards are present on the Field, all Spell Cards will be used first in a random order.

After all Spell Cards have been used, Followers will begin attacking in a random order.

The winner is determined when either your Character's HP or your opponent's Character's HP reaches 0 or below.
